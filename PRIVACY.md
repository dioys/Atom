# Atom - Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-07. Replace "Atom" / the contact line below with your own publisher name and email before you publish._

Atom is a personal assistant that runs on your own computer. We (the publisher) do **not** run any server for Atom and do **not** receive your data.

## What stays on your computer
Everything Atom saves is stored in `C:\Users\<you>\.atom` on your PC: your settings, API keys, chat memory, the files you add (and a search index of them), your skills profile, reminders and to-dos, call notes, reports and charts. Uninstalling Atom does not delete this folder; you can delete it yourself at any time.

## What is sent to other companies, and when
Atom only talks to the services **you** choose and set up with your own account or API key:

| What | Sent to | When |
|---|---|---|
| Your messages, and the text of files or screen areas you ask him about | The AI provider you pick in Settings (Groq, Google Gemini, Anthropic or OpenAI) | When you ask a question |
| Short audio clips of what you say, or of a call while **Hear** is on | The speech-to-text service (Groq Whisper) | Only while the microphone or Hear is on |
| Search words | DuckDuckGo (through the `ddgs` add-on) | When Atom checks a fact online |
| Music commands | Spotify | Only if you connect Spotify |

Each provider handles that data under its own terms and privacy policy. Atom has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Calls and screen reading
- **Hear** listens to your computer's speaker output (and Atom's call helper turns it into text). Only turn it on for calls where everyone knows, or where the law allows it. Recording or transcribing others without consent is illegal in many places; that responsibility is yours.
- Reading the screen runs on your PC (Tesseract OCR). Only the area you pick is read, and its text is sent to your AI provider only when you ask Atom to answer about it.
- Atom is silent (it does not speak) during calls so the other side cannot hear it.

## Your API keys
Keys you type in Settings are saved on your PC in `settings.json` inside the `.atom` folder. They are only sent to the matching provider.

## Your choices
Delete a chat, a file or a skill from inside Atom; delete the whole `.atom` folder to erase everything; turn off Hear, the microphone or Spotify at any time.

## Children
Atom is not directed at children under 13.
